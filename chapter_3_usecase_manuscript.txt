#### 3.1 User Authentication and Access Security Subsystem

The User Authentication and Access Security subsystem forms the primary gateway of the application. It guarantees that all interactions are strictly authenticated and authorized based on departmental boundaries.

[INSERT RENDERED IMAGE OF SUB_USECASE_1_AUTH HERE]

Figure 3.1. Use-Case Diagram of User Authentication & Access Security Subsystem

Figure 3.1 details the authentication lifecycle. When an actor logs into the system, the application automatically validates credentials against encrypted records. To protect against automated brute-force attacks, the gateway enforces strict rate limiting. Once validated, the system establishes a secure role session and enforces a Department Barrier, ensuring that faculty members and department chairs can only view and mutate schedules within their designated academic units. Users can also securely update their personal profile information and profile pictures before terminating their session via logout.

#### 3.3 AI Schedule Generation (GA Solver) Subsystem

The AI Schedule Generation subsystem encapsulates the core algorithmic engine of the application, utilizing a Genetic Algorithm (GA) to autonomously resolve multi-variable scheduling conflicts.

[INSERT RENDERED IMAGE OF SUB_USECASE_3_GA_SOLVER HERE]

Figure 3.3. Use-Case Diagram of AI Schedule Generation (GA Solver) Subsystem

Figure 3.3 demonstrates the workflow for automated schedule optimization. Administrators configure genetic parameters, including population size, elitism ratios, and mutation rates. Upon initializing the population with master curriculum data, the administrator triggers the asynchronous background GA solver. The engine calculates fitness scores by evaluating strict hard constraints (e.g., room and faculty double-booking) and soft constraints (e.g., faculty day-off preferences). Administrators can track live generation progress in real-time. Once an optimal timetable solution is achieved, administrators or superadmins can apply a Global AI-Lock to finalize and protect the master schedule from unauthorized alterations.

#### 3.6 Historical Snapshot and Archive Mode Subsystem

The Historical Snapshot and Archive Mode subsystem ensures institutional continuity and regulatory compliance by preserving immutable records of past academic terms.
