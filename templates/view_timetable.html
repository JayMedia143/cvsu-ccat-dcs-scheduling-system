Canvas Workspace Guide
1. INTERACTIVE CANVAS

Use your mouse to Drag to Pan across the infinite grid. You can Scroll to Zoom in for detail or out for a birds-eye view of all schedules.

2. NAVIGATION CONTROLS

Use the Floating HUD at the top to quickly jump to the 1st or Last schedule. The arrow buttons allow you to step through the list one by one.

3. SEARCH & SORT

Type any name or code into the Search Pill to instantly filter the canvas. You can also toggle between A-Z and Z-A sorting for easier discovery.

4. RADAR MINIMAP

The Bottom-Right Minimap shows your current position. The green box represents your viewport—use it to stay oriented within the massive workspace.

5. PRESENTATION MODE

Click the Maximize Icon on any schedule card to enter full-screen mode. Use your keyboard's Left/Right Arrows to flip through schedules while in this view.

6. HUD SHORTCUTS

Click 'Fit All' to instantly center the viewport so every active schedule card is visible on your screen at once.
