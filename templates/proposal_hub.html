Decision Hub Guide
1. Starting a Chat

Click any user on the left sidebar to start messaging. Click 'Announcements' (at the very top) for department-wide broadcasts.

2. Sending Schedule Drafts

To share a proposal, look for the (+) Plus Button at the bottom-left of the message box. Click it to select and attach your schedule draft to your message.

3. Managing Proposals

The 'Pending Review' section on the right shows all schedule proposals. There is no limit to this list, but it will automatically scroll internally if it reaches the bottom of your screen, ensuring the website stays neat. Admins can click the Green Check (Approve) or Red X (Reject) icons to finalize them.

4. Visualizing the Plan

Once you send a draft using the (+) Plus Button, a 'Schedule Draft Card' (with a calendar icon) will appear in your chat. Simply click the card to open and launch the Interactive Canvas. Once the map opens, you can left-click and hold your mouse then drag in any direction to move the map. Use your mouse scroll wheel up to zoom in (magnify details) or scroll down to zoom out.
