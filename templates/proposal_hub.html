Decision Hub Guide
1. Starting a Chat

Click any user on the left sidebar to start a private conversation. Click 'Schedule Group and Announcements' at the very top for department-wide broadcasts and official updates.

2. Sending Schedule Drafts

To share your schedule proposal, look for the (+) Plus Button at the bottom-left of the message box. Click it to select and attach your current schedule draft to your message.

{% if session.get('role') in ['admin', 'superadmin'] %}
3. Managing Proposals

The 'Pending Review' section on the right shows all submitted schedule proposals. This list will automatically scroll internally if it gets long, keeping your workspace neat and organized. Admins can click the Green Check (Approve) or Red X (Reject) icons to finalize the proposals.

4. Viewing the Schedule Map

When a draft is shared, a 'Schedule Card' with a calendar icon will appear in the chat. Simply click the card to launch the Schedule Viewer. Once the map opens, you can left-click and hold your mouse to drag and move the view. Use your mouse scroll wheel to zoom in for better detail or zoom out for a full overview.
