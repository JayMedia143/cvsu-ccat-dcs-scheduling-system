Constraints Management Guide
1. What Are Constraints?

Constraints are the "rules of the game" for the Genetic Algorithm (GA). They define what makes a schedule valid or high-quality (e.g., "No overlapping classes" or "Faculty preferred hours").

2. Priority Levels

Use the dropdowns to set a rule as Hard (HC), which must never be broken, or Soft (SC-I/II), which are goals the system tries its best to meet. Use None (NC) to disable a rule entirely.

3. Penalty Weights

Each Soft Constraint has a weight (1-100). A higher weight tells the GA that breaking this rule is very "painful" for the final score, forcing the system to prioritize it over lower-weighted rules.

4. Syncing Rules

If rules seem missing or outdated, click the 'Sync Constraints' button at the top-right. This refreshes the master list from the system core without losing your current weight settings.

5. Detailed View & Saving

Click any row to open the Right-Side Detail Panel for full descriptions. Note: Changes are not instant; you must click the Floating Green 'Save Changes' button to commit your updates.

6. Finding & Sorting
  • Search Bar: Type any part of a rule name or category; the list will update instantly as you type.
  • Funnel Icon: Click this to filter the list by a specific Priority (e.g., Hard, Soft-I, or Soft-II).
  • Sort Menu: Use the dropdown on the top-right to arrange rules by Name, Category, or Priority level.
7. Pagination

Navigate between pages using the Page Numbers or the Previous/Next buttons at the bottom. For larger lists, enter a page number into the 'Jump to' box to go there instantly. You can also use your keyboard's Left/Right arrow keys to flip through pages.
