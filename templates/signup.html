Account Creation Guide
1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONING

This portal is restricted to Superadministrators. New accounts are provisioned with 'Faculty/Department Head' privileges by default.

2. DEPARTMENTAL ISOLATION

Every user MUST be assigned to a specific department. This ensures that their scheduling workspace remains isolated and secure from other units.

3. SECURITY STANDARDS

Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2. Ensure the chosen password meets organizational complexity requirements (minimum 4 characters).

4. AUDIT COMPLIANCE

All successful registrations are automatically recorded in the System Activity Logs for security auditing and compliance tracking.
