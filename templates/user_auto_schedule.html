    Auto Generator Guide
    1. Configuration & Scope

    Select the target Semester and the Departments to include in the generation. Use the 'Fresh Start' toggle if you wish to clear any existing schedules for that semester and begin from a blank canvas.

    2. 🛡️ Capacity Assessment (Gatekeeper)

    Before the AI begins, the system performs a mandatory Resource Feasibility Check. Click 'Check Room Capacity' to analyze whether your physical rooms can accommodate the total Lecture and Lab hours requested.

    3. Understanding the 3-Level Report

    The assessment provides three perspectives: Raw (Pure capacity), Realistic (With 6hr/day limits), and Buffered (Real-world buffer). Asynchronous classes are excluded as they do not consume physical slots.

    4. Managing TBA Overflow

    If an **Overflow** is detected, some subjects will move to a 'TBA' room. You can choose to 'Continue with TBA' or return to setup to add more rooms for a perfect fit.

    5. 🧠 Hardware-Aware Optimization

    The system automatically detects your computer's performance tier. It dynamically adjusts the AI Population Size and mutation rates to ensure optimal speed on your specific device.

    6. The Real-Time Visual Grid

    The canvas maps every session in real-time: 🔴 Red (Conflicts), 🔵 Blue (Pre-assigned/Locked slots), and 🟢 Green (Optimized placement).

    7. Background Processing

    You can minimize the dashboard while generation is in progress. The AI will continue solving in the background, allowing you to browse other pages without interruption.
