Faculty Management Guide
1. Creating New Faculty

Click 'New Faculty' to add a record. Enter the Employee ID, Name, Status, and Max Hours. Be sure to include Educational Attainment, Academic Rank, and Available Days for accurate scheduling. Use '+ T.B.A.' for numbered placeholders.

2. Assigning Workload

Click the green Clipboard icon to open the 2-step Workload Wizard:

  • Step 1 (Teachable): Select the subjects the faculty is qualified to handle.
  • Step 2 (Final Load): Tick specific sections and optionally split the contact hours across different days to balance the load.

{% if session.get('role') in ['admin', 'superadmin'] %}5.{% else %}4.{% endif %} Managing & Mass Actions

Each row has icons for individual actions. To handle many records at once:

  • Eye Icon: View the detailed Faculty Profile and specialization info.
  • Calendar Icon: View the instructor's real-time Schedule.
  • Pencil Icon: Edit the faculty's details, rank, or availability days.
  • Trash Icon: Move the faculty record to the Recycle Bin.
  • Bulk Actions: Select rows or use the Header box to Select All, then use the Green Bar to bulk-delete to the Recycle Bin.
