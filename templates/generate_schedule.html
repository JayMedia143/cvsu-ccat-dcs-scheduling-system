Auto Generator Guide
1. INITIALIZATION

Before starting, select the target Semester and Departments. Use the 'Fresh Start' toggle if you want to wipe the existing schedule for that semester and start from a blank canvas.

2. THE VISUAL GRID

The canvas shows a real-time map of the timetable. Red blocks mean a Hard Conflict (e.g., overlapping rooms), Blue blocks mean Soft Violations (minor preferences), and Green blocks are perfectly placed.

3. CALCULATION THREAD

The draggable panel shows the "Calculation Thread." Monitor Throughput (Gen/Sec) to see how fast your hardware is solving the problem. "Performance Mode" uses more CPU for faster results.

4. OPERATIONS LOG

The terminal at the bottom-right tracks internal GA phases. It shows when the system is "Mutating" (trying new slots) or "Crossover" (combining good schedule parts) to resolve conflicts.

5. MINIMIZED VIEW

Click the 'Down Arrow' in the header to minimize this dashboard into a small widget. This allows you to browse other pages while the AI continues to solve the schedule in the background.

6. COMPLETION

Once the GA finds a solution or reaches its target, a 'Resolved!' screen appears. From there, you can jump directly to the Timetable to review and publish the final result.
