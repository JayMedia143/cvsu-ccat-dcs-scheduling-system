Account & System Guide
1. ACCOUNT MODIFICATION

• Change Username: Update the account's display name. A security re-authentication is required.

• Change Password: Update security credentials to ensure account integrity.

• Profile Picture: Personalize the account with a formal photo (JPG/PNG supported).

2. SYSTEM CONFIGURATION

Settings: Administrative panel used to define global operating hours, active school days, and AI-driven schedule locks.

3. ACADEMIC ARCHIVING

Finalize & Archive: A critical procedure to lock the current semester's data and migrate it to historical records. Once archived, the data becomes read-only.

4. DATA RECOVERY (RECYCLE BIN)

Restoration portal for deactivated records. The following entities can be recovered to the active state:

Courses, Sections, Students, Faculty, Rooms, and Departments.

5. SESSION TERMINATION

Sign Out: Properly ending the session to prevent unauthorized access and protect user data integrity.

Finalize Term & Create Archive

This will lock all current schedules and records for the term. It creates a final version for your archives that is 'view-only' to make sure the data stays accurate and safe from accidental changes.

Archive View Guide
1. READ-ONLY ACCESS

You are currently in "Archive Mode." All data is Read-Only to preserve the integrity of historical records. Adding, editing, or deleting entries is strictly disabled.

2. ARCHIVE IDENTIFICATION

The gold banner at the top indicates which specific Academic Year and Semester you are currently exploring. Your view is locked to this historical session.

3. DATA EXPLORATION

You can still use the 'Data' menu to browse archived lists of Courses, Faculty, and Rooms from that period. Search bars remain functional for quick data retrieval.

4. TIMETABLE & SCHEDULES

Access the 'View' menu to see the final plotted schedules for this semester. The infinite canvas remains accessible to review class distributions and layouts.

5. REPORTS GENERATION

You may still generate and print Excel reports based on this archived data through the 'Reports' menu.

6. EXITING ARCHIVE

To return to the current active semester, simply click the 'EXIT ARCHIVE' button on the right side of the gold banner.
