4. SEMESTER SELECTION

These controls are located at the top-right portion of the screen (adjacent to the profile menu). These allow users to filter dashboard statistics based on the academic term. Selecting '1st Semester' or '2nd Semester' will update all overview data to reflect only the selected academic period.

5. ACADEMIC ARCHIVES

Located beside the semester selection (represented by a Clock History icon), this feature provides access to historical schedule records. Users can review finalized schedules from previous academic years in a read-only format.
