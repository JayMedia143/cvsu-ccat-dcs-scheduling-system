Student Portal Guide
1. AUTHORIZED ACCESS

This portal is dedicated to students of CvSU-CCAT. Access is granted exclusively to registered students and authorized administrative staff for the purpose of schedule viewing.

2. SCHEDULE RETRIEVAL

To view your official class schedule, please enter your valid Student ID (e.g., 2023-0001) in the designated field. Ensure the format matches your official registration documents.

3. DATA PRIVACY

The system strictly adheres to institutional data privacy policies. Accessing schedules other than your own without proper authorization is strictly prohibited.

4. ADMINISTRATIVE MONITORING

For administrative staff, this portal allows for quick verification of student schedules. All access logs are recorded for security and auditing purposes.

5. TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE

If you encounter issues with schedule retrieval or incorrect data, please contact the DCS Department or the Registrar's Office immediately.
